Goal: Chatbot for costumors with health-issues. 
These costumors, are from rural and poor areas and don`t have any wifi connection. 
Point them out to the nearest hospital with enough capacity.

Wokflow:

Urgent cases:
1. Patient in village sends message to hospitals via broadcast
2. Hospitals server with llm sends answer back with information
3. The local application on patients device calculates (probably with ML or LLM for translstion) the best hospital for the use case

Not urgent:
Sends to local drug store


Constraints:

- The Application needs to be lightweight
- The best sent via Hash-Set or generally hashes, because the local ML calculates with the Symptoms and thats how we can transfer the data
- Message is sent ONLY via SMS
- Compatible with old devices

Must Haves:

- User friendly UX for people who are illiterate or disabled
- AI for Translation
- Algrothm for Hospital appoitance

what we solve:

- less overcrowded hospitals
- documentation record keeping burden stated
